How it works

  1. Send your request

    You'll get a reference number straight away.

  2. We plan

    A TAUI planner reviews your request and may contact you with questions.

  3. Receive a proposal

    A suggested itinerary and price, visible in your account.

  4. Accept or decline

    Nothing is booked until you agree and payment is arranged.
