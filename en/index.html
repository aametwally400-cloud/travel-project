FAQ

Good to know

All questions
Is my trip confirmed when I submit a booking request?

No. Submitting creates a booking request with a reference number. TAUI's team checks availability and contacts you with the next steps, including payment. Your booking is confirmed only after availability is confirmed and payment is received — you can follow the status in your account.

How does a custom trip work?

Tell us your destinations, dates, group size, travel style and interests. Our travel planners review your request and prepare a proposed itinerary and quote, which you can accept or decline from your account.

What do Economy, Economy Plus, Business and First Class mean?

They are TAUI comfort tiers that guide the level of accommodation, transport and service. They are not airline seat classes — flights are only included when a package says so.

How do I pay?

You pay online when you book, by bank card or mobile wallet, on the payment provider's secure page. You usually receive your booking confirmation by email as soon as the payment goes through. Never share card details by message or email.

Can I change or cancel my request?

Before you pay, you can change or cancel a trip booking from your account. After payment, contact us and we will help according to the trip's cancellation policy.

Are prices per person?

Trip prices are shown per adult in Egyptian pounds (EGP). Children's prices and group discounts, when available, are listed on each trip. Our system calculates your total and shows it before you pay.
